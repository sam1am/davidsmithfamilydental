# Dental X-Rays: Essential Diagnostic Tools

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we use state-of-the-art digital x-ray technology to safely and effectively diagnose dental conditions that aren't visible during a regular exam.

## Types of Dental X-Rays

### Bitewing X-Rays
* Check for decay between teeth
* Monitor bone levels
* Evaluate fillings

### Periapical X-Rays
* Show entire tooth structure
* Examine root tips
* Identify bone problems

### Panoramic X-Rays
* View entire mouth
* Check wisdom teeth
* Evaluate jaw joints
* Screen for abnormalities

## Benefits of Digital X-Rays

* 90% less radiation than traditional x-rays
* Immediate image availability
* Enhanced diagnostic capability
* Better patient education
* Environmentally friendly
* Easy image sharing

## When X-Rays Are Needed

* New patient exams
* Regular check-ups
* Suspected decay
* Pain or sensitivity
* Emergency situations
* Treatment planning

## Safety Measures

* Digital technology
* Lead aprons
* Thyroid collars
* Minimal exposure time
* Pregnancy considerations
* Following ALARA principles
