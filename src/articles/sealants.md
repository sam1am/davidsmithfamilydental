# Dental Sealants: Protect Against Tooth Decay

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we recommend dental sealants as an effective preventive measure to protect teeth from cavities, especially for children and teenagers.

## What Are Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are thin, protective coatings painted onto the chewing surfaces of back teeth (molars and premolars) to prevent decay in the deep grooves and pits.

## Benefits of Sealants

* Prevent 80% of cavities in back teeth
* Quick and painless application
* Long-lasting protection
* Cost-effective prevention
* No drilling required
* Invisible protection

## Who Should Get Sealants?

* Children when permanent molars emerge
* Teenagers with cavity-prone teeth
* Adults with deep grooves in teeth
* Anyone wanting extra cavity protection

## The Application Process

1. **Preparation**
   * Tooth cleaning
   * Surface drying
   * Etching solution application

2. **Application**
   * Sealant material placement
   * UV light curing
   * Bite check
   * Final inspection

## Caring for Sealants

* Regular brushing and flossing
* Avoid sticky foods
* Regular dental check-ups
* Report any changes
* Can last several years
