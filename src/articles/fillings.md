# Dental Fillings: Restoring Your Teeth with Precision

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we provide high-quality dental fillings to repair cavities and restore damaged teeth. Our modern techniques and materials ensure durable, natural-looking results.

## Types of Dental Fillings

### Composite (Tooth-Colored) Fillings
* Match natural tooth color
* Bond directly to tooth
* Preserve more tooth structure
* Ideal for visible teeth
* Mercury-free

### Amalgam Fillings
* Extremely durable
* Cost-effective
* Ideal for back teeth
* Long-lasting
* Time-tested solution

## When Are Fillings Needed?

* Cavity repair
* Cracked or broken teeth
* Worn teeth
* Small gaps between teeth
* Old filling replacement
* Minor tooth decay

## The Filling Process

1. **Examination:**
   * Visual inspection
   * X-rays if needed
   * Discussion of options

2. **Procedure:**
   * Area numbed for comfort
   * Decay removal
   * Tooth preparation
   * Filling placement
   * Shaping and polishing

## Benefits of Modern Fillings

* Stop decay progression
* Restore tooth function
* Prevent further damage
* Improve appearance
* Long-lasting results
* Comfortable procedure

## Caring for Your Fillings

* Brush twice daily
* Floss regularly
* Avoid excessive force
* Regular dental check-ups
* Professional cleanings
* Avoid hard foods

## Making the Right Choice

We'll help you choose the best filling material based on:
* Location of cavity
* Extent of decay
* Insurance coverage
* Personal preferences
* Aesthetic goals
* Durability needs
