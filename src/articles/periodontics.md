# Periodontics: Maintaining Healthy Gums and Bone

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our periodontal services focus on preventing, diagnosing, and treating gum disease and other conditions affecting the supporting structures of teeth.

## Our Periodontal Services

### Preventive Care
* Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing)
* Maintenance therapy
* Risk assessment
* Patient education

### Surgical Procedures
* Gum grafting
* Crown lengthening
* Pocket reduction
* Bone regeneration

## Signs of Periodontal Disease

* Bleeding gums
* Swollen or tender gums
* Receding gums
* Loose teeth
* Persistent bad breath
* Changes in bite
* Pus between teeth and gums

## Treatment Stages

1. **Initial Evaluation**
   * Comprehensive examination
   * X-rays and measurements
   * Treatment planning

2. **Non-Surgical Treatment**
   * Deep cleaning
   * Antibiotic therapy
   * Home care instruction

3. **Surgical Treatment**
   * Advanced cases
   * Tissue regeneration
   * Cosmetic procedures

## Prevention Tips

* Regular dental cleanings
* Proper brushing technique
* Daily flossing
* Healthy diet
* Tobacco cessation
* Regular check-ups
