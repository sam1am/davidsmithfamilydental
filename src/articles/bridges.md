# Dental Bridges: Restore Your Smile with a Natural-Looking Solution

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we understand how missing teeth can affect both your confidence and oral health. Dental bridges are an excellent solution for replacing one or more missing teeth, helping restore your beautiful smile and maintain proper dental function.

## What Is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is a fixed prosthetic device that literally "bridges" the gap created by one or more missing teeth. It consists of one or more artificial teeth (pontics) anchored in place by dental crowns attached to the natural teeth on either side of the gap.

## Benefits of Dental Bridges

* Restores your natural smile
* Maintains proper bite alignment
* Prevents remaining teeth from shifting
* Improves speaking and chewing ability
* Supports facial structure
* Long-lasting and durable solution

## Types of Dental Bridges

We offer several types of bridges at our Salt Lake City practice:

1. **Traditional Bridges**: Most common type, supported by crowns on adjacent teeth
2. **Cantilever Bridges**: Used when there are healthy teeth on only one side
3. **Maryland Bridges**: More conservative option using a metal framework bonded to existing teeth

## The Bridge Process

Getting a dental bridge typically requires two visits to our office. During the first visit, we prepare the adjacent teeth and take impressions. At the second visit, we permanently cement your custom-made bridge in place, ensuring a perfect fit and natural appearance.

## Caring for Your Bridge

With proper care and maintenance, your dental bridge can last 10-15 years or longer. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential for maintaining your bridge and overall oral health.
