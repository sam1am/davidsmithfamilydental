# Scaling and Root Planing: Deep Cleaning for Gum Health

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we offer scaling and root planing as an effective treatment for periodontal disease, helping patients maintain healthy gums and prevent tooth loss.

## What is Scaling and Root Planing?

This deep cleaning procedure removes plaque, tartar, and bacterial toxins both above and below the gum line, smoothing root surfaces to promote healing and prevent future bacterial attachment.

## When Is It Needed?

* Presence of deep periodontal pockets
* Bleeding or inflamed gums
* Persistent bad breath
* Visible tartar buildup
* Early stages of gum disease
* Receding gums

## The Procedure

1. **Initial Assessment**
   * Periodontal measurements
   * X-rays if needed
   * Treatment planning

2. **The Treatment**
   * Local anesthesia for comfort
   * Thorough cleaning below gumline
   * Root surface smoothing
   * Antimicrobial application

## Benefits

* Stops progression of gum disease
* Reduces inflammation
* Promotes gum reattachment
* Eliminates bad breath
* Protects tooth roots
* Prevents tooth loss

## Aftercare

* Temporary sensitivity normal
* Follow special cleaning instructions
* Use prescribed rinses
* Schedule follow-up visits
* Maintain good oral hygiene
