# Understanding ClearCorrect Clear Aligners at David Smith Family Dentistry

## What Are ClearCorrect Aligners?
ClearCorrect aligners are custom-made, clear plastic trays that gradually straighten your teeth. They offer a nearly invisible alternative to traditional braces, allowing you to achieve a beautiful smile discreetly.

## Benefits of ClearCorrect
* Nearly invisible appearance
* Removable for eating and cleaning
* No food restrictions
* Comfortable to wear
* Fewer office visits required
* No metal brackets or wires
* Easy maintenance of oral hygiene

## How ClearCorrect Works
1. Custom Treatment Planning
   * Digital scanning of your teeth
   * Creation of 3D treatment plan
   * Preview of expected results

2. The Aligner Process
   * Series of custom-made clear aligners
   * Each set worn for about 2 weeks
   * Gradual tooth movement
   * Regular progress checks

## Treatable Conditions
* Crowded teeth
* Gaps between teeth
* Overbite
* Underbite
* Crossbite
* Minor to moderate misalignment

## The Treatment Process

### Initial Consultation
* Comprehensive oral examination
* Digital impressions
* Discussion of treatment goals
* Custom treatment planning

### During Treatment
* Wear aligners 22 hours per day
* Change to new aligners every 2 weeks
* Regular check-ups every 6-8 weeks
* Track progress toward your ideal smile

### After Treatment
* Retainer fitting
* Long-term retention plan
* Maintenance instructions

## Caring for Your Aligners
* Remove when eating or drinking (except water)
* Clean aligners daily
* Store in protective case
* Maintain good oral hygiene
* Keep regular dental check-ups

## Treatment Duration
Treatment length varies based on:
* Complexity of tooth movement needed
* Compliance with wearing aligners
* Individual response to treatment

Most cases complete in 6-18 months, though treatment times vary by individual.

## Is ClearCorrect Right for You?
ClearCorrect is ideal for teens and adults seeking a discreet orthodontic solution. They're particularly popular among:
* Working professionals
* Active individuals
* Those with social considerations
* Anyone seeking a subtle orthodontic option
