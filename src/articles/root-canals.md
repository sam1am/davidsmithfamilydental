# Root Canal Therapy: Saving Natural Teeth

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our root canal treatments help save damaged or infected teeth, eliminating pain while preserving your natural smile.

## What is a Root Canal?

A root canal is a procedure that removes infected or damaged pulp from inside a tooth, cleaning and sealing the space to prevent future infection.

## When is a Root Canal Needed?

* Severe tooth decay
* Deep cracks or chips
* Repeated dental procedures
* Trauma to the tooth
* Persistent sensitivity
* Abscess formation

## The Root Canal Process

1. **Diagnosis**
   * X-rays and testing
   * Treatment planning
   * Discussion of options

2. **Procedure**
   * Local anesthesia
   * Removal of infected pulp
   * Cleaning and shaping
   * Filling and sealing

3. **Restoration**
   * Crown placement
   * Final adjustments
   * Follow-up care

## Benefits of Root Canal Therapy

* Saves natural tooth
* Eliminates pain
* Restores function
* Prevents extraction
* Cost-effective long-term
* Maintains natural smile

## After Your Root Canal

* Temporary sensitivity normal
* Avoid chewing on treated tooth
* Maintain good oral hygiene
* Regular dental check-ups
* Contact us with concerns
