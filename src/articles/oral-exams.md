# Comprehensive Oral Exams: Your Path to Dental Health

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we provide thorough oral examinations to assess your overall dental health and catch potential problems early. Our comprehensive approach ensures nothing is overlooked.

## What We Examine

### Dental Structures
* Teeth condition
* Existing restorations
* Bite alignment
* Jaw joint function
* Gum health
* Bone structure

### Oral Health Screening
* Cavity detection
* Gum disease assessment
* Oral cancer screening
* TMJ evaluation
* Bite analysis
* Growth patterns

## Advanced Technology

* Digital X-rays
* Intraoral cameras
* 3D imaging when needed
* Cavity detection tools
* Periodontal probing
* Documentation systems

## Examination Process

1. **Health History Review**
   * Medical conditions
   * Medications
   * Previous dental work
   * Current concerns
   * Family history

2. **Visual Examination**
   * Tooth-by-tooth inspection
   * Gum evaluation
   * Soft tissue check
   * Bite assessment
   * Restoration check

3. **Diagnostic Tools**
   * X-rays as needed
   * Cavity detection
   * Periodontal measurements
   * Cancer screening
   * TMJ evaluation

* Future planning
