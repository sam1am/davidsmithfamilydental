# Prosthodontics: Specialized Care for Complex Dental Restoration

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our prosthodontic services focus on the restoration and replacement of missing or damaged teeth using advanced techniques and materials.

## Our Prosthodontic Services

### Fixed Solutions
* Crowns
* Bridges
* Veneers
* Implant restorations

### Removable Solutions
* Complete dentures
* Partial dentures
* Implant-supported dentures
* Temporary prosthetics

## Advanced Procedures

* Full mouth reconstruction
* TMJ treatment
* Aesthetic enhancement
* Complex implant cases
* Occlusal rehabilitation

* Long-lasting results
