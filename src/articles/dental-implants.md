# Dental Implants: The Gold Standard in Tooth Replacement

At David Smith Family Dental, we offer state-of-the-art dental implants to provide our Salt Lake City patients with the most permanent and natural-looking solution for missing teeth.

## What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They serve as anchors for replacement teeth, providing a sturdy foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures.

## Benefits of Dental Implants

* Look and function like natural teeth
* Prevent bone loss in the jaw
* Can last a lifetime with proper care
* No impact on adjacent teeth
* Improve speaking and eating ability
* Maintain facial structure
* Boost confidence and self-esteem

## The Implant Process

1. **Consultation and Planning:**
   * Comprehensive exam
   * 3D imaging
   * Treatment plan development

2. **Implant Placement:**
   * Surgical insertion of implant
   * Healing period (3-6 months)

3. **Restoration:**
   * Attachment of abutment
   * Placement of final crown or prosthetic

## Candidacy for Implants

Good candidates typically have:
* Healthy gums
* Adequate bone density
* Good overall health
* Commitment to oral hygiene

## Types of Implant Solutions

* Single tooth replacement
* Multiple tooth replacement
* Full arch replacement
* Implant-supported dentures

## Caring for Your Implants

* Brush twice daily
* Floss regularly
* Attend regular check-ups
* Avoid tobacco products
* Maintain good oral hygiene
