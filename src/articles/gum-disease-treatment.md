# Gum Disease Treatment: Protecting Your Oral Health

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we offer comprehensive periodontal (gum disease) treatment to help maintain your oral health and prevent tooth loss. Early intervention is key to successful treatment.

## Understanding Gum Disease

### Gingivitis (Early Stage)
* Red, swollen gums
* Bleeding while brushing
* Bad breath
* Reversible with treatment

### Periodontitis (Advanced Stage)
* Receding gums
* Loose teeth
* Bone loss
* Requires professional intervention

## Our Treatment Options

### Non-Surgical Treatments
* Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing)
* Antibiotic therapy
* Laser therapy
* Medicated mouth rinses
* Enhanced home care protocols

### Surgical Treatments
* Pocket reduction
* Gum grafting
* Bone grafting
* Tissue regeneration

## Prevention and Maintenance

* Regular dental cleanings
* Proper brushing technique
* Daily flossing
* Antimicrobial rinses
* Tobacco cessation
* Dietary modifications

## Risk Factors

* Poor oral hygiene
* Smoking
* Diabetes
* Genetics
* Certain medications
* Hormonal changes
* Stress

## Benefits of Treatment

* Prevents tooth loss
* Reduces inflammation
* Improves breath
* Protects overall health
* Saves money long-term
* Maintains natural smile
