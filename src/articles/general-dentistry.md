# General Dentistry: Your Path to Optimal Oral Health

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our comprehensive general dentistry services form the foundation of excellent oral health. We focus on prevention, early detection, and maintaining your healthy smile for life.

## Our General Dentistry Services

### Preventive Care
* Regular cleanings
* Comprehensive examinations
* Digital X-rays
* Oral cancer screenings
* Fluoride treatments
* Dental sealants

### Restorative Services
* Fillings
* Crowns and bridges
* Root canal therapy
* Dentures and partials
* Basic extractions
* Emergency dental care

### Maintenance Care
* Periodontal treatments
* Cavity prevention
* Night guards
* Sports mouth guards
* Sensitivity treatments

## Why Regular Dental Care Matters

* Prevents serious dental problems
* Saves money long-term
* Maintains overall health
* Catches issues early
* Preserves natural teeth
* Prevents pain and discomfort
