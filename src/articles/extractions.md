# Tooth Extractions: Gentle and Professional Care

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we understand that tooth extraction can seem intimidating. Our experienced team provides comfortable, efficient extraction services when saving a tooth isn't possible.

## When Are Extractions Necessary?

* Severe tooth decay
* Advanced gum disease
* Broken teeth beyond repair
* Impacted wisdom teeth
* Overcrowded teeth
* Preparation for orthodontic treatment
* Baby teeth that haven't fallen out naturally

## Types of Extractions

### Simple Extractions
* For visible teeth
* Performed under local anesthesia
* Straightforward procedure

### Surgical Extractions
* For impacted or broken teeth
* May require incision in gum tissue
* More complex procedure
* Often needed for wisdom teeth

## The Extraction Process

1. **Preparation:**
   * Comprehensive examination
   * X-rays
   * Review of medical history
   * Discussion of sedation options

2. **Procedure:**
   * Area thoroughly numbed
   * Gentle removal of tooth
   * Placement of gauze
   * Post-operative instructions

## Recovery and Aftercare

* Follow all post-operative instructions
* Rest for 24 hours
* Apply ice packs as directed
* Eat soft foods
* Avoid straws
* Keep the area clean
* Take prescribed medications
* Schedule follow-up if needed

## Replacement Options

After healing, we offer several tooth replacement options:
* Dental implants
* Bridges
* Partial dentures

## Your Comfort is Our Priority

We offer several comfort options:
* Local anesthesia
* Sedation options
* Gentle techniques
* Comprehensive aftercare
* Emergency care if needed
