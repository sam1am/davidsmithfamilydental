# Oral Surgery: Advanced Dental Care for Complex Issues

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our oral surgery services provide solutions for patients requiring more advanced dental procedures beyond routine care.

## Common Oral Surgery Procedures

### Tooth Extractions
* Wisdom teeth removal
* Severely damaged teeth
* Orthodontic preparation
* Impacted teeth

### Bone Grafting
* Preparation for dental implants
* Socket preservation
* Ridge augmentation
* Sinus lifts

### Surgical Procedures
* Apicoectomy
* Frenectomy
* Biopsy
* Treatment of oral infections

## What to Expect

1. **Pre-Surgery:**
   * Comprehensive examination
   * Medical history review
   * Treatment planning
   * Pre-operative instructions

2. **During Surgery:**
   * Local anesthesia or sedation
   * Sterile surgical environment
   * Expert care and monitoring

3. **Post-Surgery:**
   * Detailed aftercare instructions
   * Pain management guidance
   * Follow-up appointments
   * 24/7 emergency support

## Recovery and Aftercare

* Follow post-operative instructions
* Take prescribed medications
* Apply ice as directed
* Maintain soft food diet
* Keep surgical site clean
* Attend follow-up appointments
