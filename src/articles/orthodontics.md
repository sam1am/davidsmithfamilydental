# Orthodontics: Creating Beautiful, Straight Smiles

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, our orthodontic services help patients achieve properly aligned teeth and jaws for both aesthetic and functional benefits.

## Treatment Options

### Traditional Braces
* Metal brackets and wires
* Colored bands available
* Effective for complex cases
* Suitable for all ages

### Clear Aligners
* Nearly invisible treatment
* Removable for eating and cleaning
* Comfortable fit
* Fewer office visits

### Other Appliances
* Palatal expanders
* Space maintainers
* Retainers
* Bite plates

## Common Issues We Treat

* Crowded teeth
* Gaps between teeth
* Overbite
* Underbite
* Crossbite
* Open bite
* Misaligned jaw

## Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

* Improved smile aesthetics
* Better oral hygiene
* Enhanced chewing function
* Proper speech development
* Reduced risk of dental problems
* Increased self-confidence

## Treatment Process

1. Initial consultation
2. Diagnostic records
3. Treatment plan development
4. Active treatment phase
5. Retention phase

## Care During Treatment

* Regular orthodontic check-ups
* Proper oral hygiene
* Dietary modifications
* Protective measures during sports
* Following treatment instructions
