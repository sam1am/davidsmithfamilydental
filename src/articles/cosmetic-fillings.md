# Cosmetic Fillings: The Modern Solution for Tooth Decay

At David Smith Family Dental, we provide tooth-colored composite fillings that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. These modern cosmetic fillings offer both aesthetic and functional benefits, making them an excellent choice for treating cavities while maintaining a beautiful smile.

## What Are Cosmetic Fillings?

Cosmetic fillings, also known as composite fillings, are made from a mixture of plastic and fine glass particles. Unlike traditional silver amalgam fillings, these tooth-colored restorations are virtually invisible and provide a natural-looking solution for treating dental decay.

## Benefits of Cosmetic Fillings

* Match your natural tooth color perfectly
* Require less removal of healthy tooth structure
* Bond directly to your tooth for added strength
* Mercury-free and environmentally friendly
* Can be repaired if damaged
* Versatile enough to fix chips and small gaps

## The Filling Process

Getting a cosmetic filling at our Salt Lake City office is a straightforward procedure:

1. The damaged area is cleaned and prepared
2. The composite material is carefully layered into the cavity
3. A special light hardens each layer
4. The filling is shaped and polished to match your bite

## Durability and Longevity

With proper care and maintenance, composite fillings can last for many years. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups will help extend the life of your fillings and maintain your oral health.

## Why Choose Composite Over Amalgam?

Modern composite fillings offer several advantages over traditional silver amalgam:

* More aesthetically pleasing
* Preserve more natural tooth structure
* No risk of metal sensitivity
* Better seal against decay
* No gray discoloration of the tooth
* Environmentally conscious choice

## Caring for Your Cosmetic Fillings

To maintain your cosmetic fillings:
* Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
* Floss daily
* Avoid excessive force when chewing
* Schedule regular dental check-ups
* Avoid staining substances like tobacco
