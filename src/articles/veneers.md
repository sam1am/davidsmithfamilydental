# Porcelain Veneers: Transform Your Smile

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we offer custom porcelain veneers to help patients achieve the beautiful, natural-looking smile they've always wanted.

## What Are Veneers?

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells of ceramic material bonded to the front of teeth to improve their appearance. They're custom-made to match your natural teeth while hiding imperfections.

## Conditions Veneers Can Fix

* Discolored teeth
* Worn down teeth
* Chipped or broken teeth
* Misaligned teeth
* Gaps between teeth
* Irregularly shaped teeth
* Uneven teeth

## The Veneer Process

1. **Consultation**
   * Discussion of goals
   * Treatment planning
   * Digital smile preview

2. **Preparation**
   * Minimal tooth reduction
   * Impressions
   * Temporary veneers

3. **Final Placement**
   * Custom fitting
   * Bonding
   * Final adjustments

## Benefits of Veneers

* Natural-looking results
* Stain-resistant
* Durable and long-lasting
* Minimal tooth alteration
* Immediate results
* Custom-designed smile

## Caring for Your Veneers

* Regular brushing and flossing
* Avoid excessive force
* Wear night guard if recommended
* Regular dental check-ups
* Avoid staining substances
