# Dental Crowns: Restore and Protect Your Teeth

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we provide high-quality dental crowns to restore damaged teeth and protect them from further decay. A crown acts as a protective cap, covering the entire visible portion of your tooth while maintaining a natural appearance.

## What Are Dental Crowns?

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that fit over damaged or weakened teeth. They can be made from various materials, including porcelain, ceramic, metal alloys, or a combination of materials, depending on your specific needs and preferences.

## When Are Crowns Needed?

* Protect a weak tooth from breaking
* Restore an already broken tooth
* Cover a tooth with a large filling
* Hold a dental bridge in place
* Cover misshapen or severely discolored teeth
* Protect teeth after root canal treatment

## The Crown Process

Getting a crown typically involves two visits:

1. **First Visit:**
   * Tooth preparation and shaping
   * Digital impressions taken
   * Temporary crown placed

2. **Second Visit:**
   * Temporary crown removed
   * Permanent crown fitted and cemented
   * Final adjustments made

## Caring for Your Crown

With proper care, your crown can last 10-15 years or longer:
* Brush twice daily and floss regularly
* Avoid hard foods and ice chewing
* Maintain regular dental check-ups
* Use a night guard if you grind your teeth

## Benefits of Dental Crowns

* Restore tooth function and appearance
* Protect weakened teeth
* Improve bite alignment
* Long-lasting solution
* Natural-looking results
