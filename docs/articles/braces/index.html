Understanding Braces at David Smith Family Dentistry

What Are Braces?

Braces are orthodontic devices designed to correct misaligned teeth and jaws. They work by applying consistent pressure over time to gradually move teeth into their proper positions.

Types of Braces We Offer

Traditional Metal Braces

  • Made from high-grade stainless steel
  • Brackets attached to teeth, connected by wire
  • Most economical option
  • Highly effective for various orthodontic issues

Clear Ceramic Braces

  • Function like metal braces but use tooth-colored or clear materials
  • Less visible than metal braces
  • Popular with adult patients and teens
  • Similar treatment time to metal braces

Common Issues Braces Can Fix

  • Crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbite or underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Misaligned teeth

The Treatment Process

Initial Steps

  • Thorough dental examination
  • X-rays and dental impressions
  • Creation of personalized treatment plan

During Treatment

  • Regular adjustments every 4-6 weeks
  • Progress monitoring
  • Oral hygiene instruction and care

Post-Treatment

  • Retainer fitting and wear instructions
  • Follow-up visits to ensure lasting results

Caring for Braces

  • Brush thoroughly after meals
  • Use special flossing tools
  • Avoid certain foods that could damage braces
  • Maintain regular dental check-ups
  • Wear rubber bands as directed

Treatment Duration

Treatment time varies depending on:

  • Severity of orthodontic issues
  • Type of braces chosen
  • Patient compliance
  • Individual response to treatment

Most patients wear braces for 18-24 months, though treatment times can be shorter or longer based on individual needs.
