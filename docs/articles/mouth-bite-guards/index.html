Mouth Guards & Bite Guards: Protect Your Smile

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we create custom-fitted mouth guards and bite guards to protect your teeth from damage due to sports activities and nighttime grinding (bruxism).

Types of Guards

Sports Guards

  • Custom-fitted for comfort
  • Superior protection
  • Better breathing and speaking
  • Multiple color options
  • Durable materials
  • Professional grade

Night Guards

  • Prevents teeth grinding
  • Reduces jaw pain
  • Protects dental work
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Custom-fitted design
  • Long-lasting materials

Benefits of Custom Guards

  • Perfect fit for maximum protection
  • More comfortable than store-bought options
  • Better durability
  • Improved breathing
  • Enhanced protection
  • Professional-grade materials

When You Need a Guard

Sports Protection

  • Contact sports
  • High-impact activities
  • Team sports
  • Recreational activities
  • Training sessions
  • Competition events

Night Guard Needs

  • Teeth grinding
  • Jaw clenching
  • TMJ symptoms
  • Headaches
  • Worn teeth
  • Dental work protection

The Fitting Process

  1. Digital impressions
  2. Custom fabrication
  3. Professional fitting
  4. Comfort adjustments
  5. Care instructions

Caring for Your Guard

  • Clean after each use
  • Store in protective case
  • Regular maintenance checks
  • Replace when worn
  • Professional cleaning
  • Regular inspections
