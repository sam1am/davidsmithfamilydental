Dentures and Partials: Complete Smile Solutions

At David Smith Family Dental in Salt Lake City, we offer both complete dentures and partial dentures to help restore your smile, regardless of how many teeth you're missing. Our custom-fitted dental prosthetics are designed to improve your quality of life and restore proper oral function.

Complete Dentures

Complete dentures are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace all teeth in either the upper or lower jaw. They're custom-made to fit your mouth comfortably while providing a natural-looking smile.

Types of Complete Dentures

Conventional Dentures

  • Placed after healing from tooth extraction
  • Most accurate fit
  • Traditional option

Immediate Dentures

  • Placed immediately after extraction
  • Allows you to never go without teeth
  • May need adjustment as healing occurs

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are an excellent solution when you have some natural teeth remaining. They consist of replacement teeth attached to a gum-colored base, connected by a framework for support.

Types of Partial Dentures

Cast Metal Partials

  • Most durable option
  • Excellent fit
  • Thinner and more comfortable

Flexible Partials

  • Metal-free
  • More comfortable
  • Virtually invisible clasps
  • Ideal for metal allergies

Acrylic Partials

  • Economical option
  • Can be modified easily
  • Often used as a temporary solution

Benefits of Dentures and Partials

  • Restore ability to eat and speak
  • Support facial muscles
  • Improve appearance
  • Boost self-confidence
  • Prevent remaining teeth from shifting (partials)
  • Cost-effective solution
  • Can be removed for cleaning

The Denture Process

  1. Initial consultation and impressions
  2. Wax try-in for fit and appearance
  3. Final adjustments
  4. Denture delivery and education
  5. Follow-up appointments as needed

Adjusting to Your New Teeth

  • Initial adjustment period of several weeks
  • Start with soft foods
  • Practice speaking
  • Follow care instructions
  • Regular check-ups for adjustments

Caring for Your Dentures or Partials

  • Clean daily
  • Remove for sleeping
  • Soak overnight
  • Handle with care
  • Brush remaining natural teeth (for partial wearers)
  • Regular dental check-ups
  • Replace every 5-7 years

Making the Right Choice

During your consultation at our Salt Lake City office, we'll help determine whether complete or partial dentures are the best solution for your specific needs. Factors we consider include:

  • Number of missing teeth
  • Health of remaining teeth
  • Bone structure
  • Budget considerations
  • Lifestyle factors
  • Long-term goals
